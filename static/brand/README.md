# Brand assets

`psc-sahabat.svg` is a transparent vector reconstruction of the PSC SAHABAT emblem supplied by the project owner on 22 October 2026. It preserves the magenta adult figure, blue child, and heart motif. The SVG is the website and scalable favicon source; PNG variants are rendered at 32, 180 (Apple touch icon), 512, and 2048 pixels. The PNGs contain no background or generated lettering. This reconstruction is not a replacement for an institution-approved master if one becomes available.

`puskesmas-logo.png` is the Puskesmas emblem retrieved on 19 October 2026 from an official government Puskesmas Muncan page:

https://puskesmasmuncan.lomboktengahkab.go.id/uploads/images/Logo_Puskesmas_Tanpa_Background.png

The institution name, UPT Puskesmas Suranadi, was verified on its official Lombok Barat government website:

https://puskesmassuranadi-dikes.lombokbaratkab.go.id/

No unverified village list, service contact, or real victim record has been added. The owner should supply approved institutional details before operational publication.
